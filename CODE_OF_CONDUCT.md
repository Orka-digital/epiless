# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome people with different backgrounds, levels of experience and accessibility needs. Critique ideas and implementation choices without attacking people. Respect privacy and requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate misrepresentation, publishing private information and repeatedly disrupting collaboration are not acceptable. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where someone represents epi-less.

Report concerns privately to a listed maintainer through their published contact route. Do not put private evidence in a public issue. If a maintainer is involved, contact another maintainer where one is available. Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy, consider context and explain any action to affected participants when possible.

Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a temporary restriction or removal from project spaces. The response should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. A participant may ask a different available maintainer to review a decision. This policy is written for this project and does not claim a staffed independent moderation service.
